Privacy Policy

Save Our Spends (SoS) • Effective Date: November 4, 2026

1. Overview & Architecture

Save Our Spends ("SoS", "we", "our", or "the app"), accessible at https://spends.notafakecloud.uk, is a decentralized, serverless web application designed to facilitate group expense sharing and debt simplification.

Fundamental Principle: 100% Client-Side Architecture. Save Our Spends does not operate any proprietary backend application servers or central databases. All computation, financial calculations, and split logic run directly in your web browser. All persistent data is stored strictly inside user-owned Google Sheets spreadsheets in your own Google Drive.

2. Information We Access and How It Is Used

Save Our Spends integrates with Google Identity Services and Google Drive / Google Sheets APIs using the principle of least-privilege access.

Google User Profile Information (userinfo.profile & userinfo.email)

  • Information Accessed: Your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Purpose: To authenticate you, identify you to fellow group members, display your avatar in expense split lists, and assign expense records to your personal ledger tab (e.g. Exp_<email>).
  • Storage: Cached locally on your device in browser storage (localStorage) to maintain your active session. We never transmit your profile data to any external server.

Google Drive & Sheets Access (drive.file)

  • Information Accessed: Per-file access strictly limited to spreadsheets created by Save Our Spends or files explicitly selected by you using the Google Drive Picker.
  • Least-Privilege Guarantee: We never request full Google Drive access (drive or drive.readonly). The app cannot view, modify, or list any other files, documents, or photos in your Google Drive.
  • Purpose: To read and write expense rows, members list, settlements, and summary balances within your designated expense spreadsheets.

3. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Save Our Spends' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. How We Protect and Handle Your Data

  • Zero Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, monetize, trade, or transfer any user data, financial data, or Google user information to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • No Advertisements: Save Our Spends is completely ad-free. Your data is never used for advertising, marketing, or behavioral tracking.
  • No AI/ML Training: Your Google user data and spreadsheet contents are never used to train, tune, or develop generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
  • Direct Secure Communication: All network requests travel directly between your web browser and official Google API endpoints (https://www.googleapis.com) encrypted via TLS/HTTPS. No intermediate proxy or telemetry service intercepts your requests.

5. Local Data Storage & Offline Caching

To deliver instant startup and offline viewing capabilities as a Progressive Web App (PWA), Save Our Spends caches certain data locally on your device using:

  • localStorage & sessionStorage: User preferences, UI theme setting, and temporary OAuth authentication tokens.
  • IndexedDB (via idb-keyval): Encrypted local cache of your group spreadsheets to allow viewing expenses when offline.

You can clear this local data at any time through your browser settings or by clicking "Clear Cache & Reload" in the application.

6. Data Ownership, Retention, and Deletion

Because all financial records and group spreadsheets are created directly on your Google Drive:

  • Full Ownership: You retain complete ownership and control over all spreadsheets and expense entries.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete any transaction, group, or spreadsheet at any time directly in Google Sheets or Google Drive. Once deleted from your Drive, the app has no ability to recover or retain the data.
  • Revoking App Permissions: You can revoke Save Our Spends' access to your Google account at any time by visiting Google Account Security Settings > Third-party apps & services.

7. Children's Privacy

Save Our Spends does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
